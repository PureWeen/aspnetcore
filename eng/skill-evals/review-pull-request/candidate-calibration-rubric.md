# Original line-77 calibration rubric

Grade each case conjunctively against the frozen source labels. Process success, an
aggregate score, expected vocabulary, or a no-finding conclusion cannot compensate for a
wrong causal explanation.

## Dimensions

1. **Mechanism:** Determine whether the exact post-check callback/store interleaving is
   legal under the packet premise and whether it restores the stale cache.
2. **Applicability:** Use only supplied evidence to establish or identify the missing
   Blazor host/provider support contract.
3. **Old/head comparison:** Determine whether the material effect is introduced by the
   PR or already exists under the same premise.
4. **PR actionability:** Decide whether the candidate is a justified new finding on this
   PR, not whether separate hardening could ever be useful.
5. **Evidence discipline:** Do not use omitted facts, deduplication alone, maintainer
   authority, expected labels, or a proposed guide sentence as the answer.
6. **Runtime fidelity:** Require the native skill load and complete reads of both staged
   routed guides. Missing load/read evidence makes the attempt inconclusive.

The supporting model judge is not the source of truth. Inspect every subject output and
judge rationale against `candidate-calibration-labels.json`. Preserve unresolved
disagreements as inconclusive.
