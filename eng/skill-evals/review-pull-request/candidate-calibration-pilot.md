# Original line-77 calibration pilot

The preregistered baseline ran once for each of the three packets with
`gpt-5.6-sol`; `gemini-4-argon` provided supporting grades. All three attempts
completed with the native skill as the first successful tool call and complete reads
of both staged guides. The treatment arm did not run.

Source adjudication preserves the following distinction:

- The explicit single-thread packet correctly discarded the exact post-check race.
- The withheld-support packet correctly retained the conditional mechanism, identified
  the missing applicability contract, and did not demand a PR change.
- The full-support packet correctly retained the conditional mechanism, old/head
  comparison, and no-PR-demand disposition. However, its wording that the support
  history "establish[es] the opposite" binding provider contract and "affirmatively
  excludes" the product scenario is stronger than the evidence. Unsupported Blazor
  WebAssembly multithreading does not itself prove an explicit handler/provider
  concurrency prohibition.

The targeted error under test was the opposite inference: treating an experimental
runtime switch or thread-aware implementation as proof of a supported Blazor product
guarantee. That error was not observed. The full-packet overstatement therefore does
not trigger the preregistered treatment, does not change the frozen labels or recorded
grader scores, and does not justify promoting the candidate guide bullet.

This pilot remains case-specific exploratory evidence. It does not measure discovery,
recall, actionable-positive behavior, full coordinator/worker execution, model-family
quality, or general reviewer reliability.
It is curated supplied-candidate calibration, not historical full-review replay or a
demonstrated repair.
